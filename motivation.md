# Cover Letter

Dear Ms. Weiss,

What draws me to advisory work is the point where analysis becomes a decision. During my studies and internships, I found that I most enjoy assignments where numbers need to be translated into a clear recommendation for a client, whether that means defending a valuation, pressure-testing a model, or presenting a concise view under time pressure. That is why I am applying for the Off-Cycle Analyst role at Edelweiss Advisory AG.

I am currently completing a master's in Banking and Finance at the University of St. Gallen after a bachelor's in Business Administration from the University of Zurich and an exchange semester at Copenhagen Business School. Across those programs, I built a strong base in valuation, accounting, corporate finance and data-driven decision making. I have consistently looked for settings where that technical work is paired with communication and ownership.

At Helvetia Capital Partners, I supported live sell-side and refinancing mandates by updating financial models, preparing valuation materials and drafting presentation content for senior bankers. I also helped manage diligence workstreams and comparable-company analyses used in client discussions. Earlier, at Limmat Advisory, I worked on valuation engagements for founder-led businesses, where I learned to move carefully through imperfect data, structure assumptions clearly and explain conclusions in a way that remains practical for management teams.

Outside internships, I lead the industrials coverage team at the Alpine Student Investment Fund and serve as Conference Director for the Zurich Finance Forum. Those roles have strengthened my ability to organize work, coordinate with others and present clear investment views to demanding audiences. They also confirmed that I work best in high-expectation environments where precision and pace both matter.

Edelweiss Advisory stands out to me because of its focus on high-trust, analytically rigorous advice for Swiss and cross-border clients. I would value the opportunity to contribute with disciplined execution, strong attention to detail and a calm approach to teamwork while continuing to develop as an analyst in a demanding environment.

Thank you for your consideration. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I could contribute to your team.

Sincerely,
